What it's for and how it works:
When an important action is performed on your account, such as changing your password or withdrawing funds, and we need to verify that it is really you, the Authenticator generates a one-time code for verification.
We will send you the code in a push notification, and all you'll need to do is enter it in the confirmation field. This is much quicker, more convenient and secure than sending the code by SMS.
A history of such actions will be available in this section.
How to enable:
  • You must have activated your phone number.
  • The app must have permission to display notifications.
The Authenticator is only available to customers who have registered and logged in.
